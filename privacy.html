Privacy

DRAFT (owner ke liye): ye ek aam template hai, kisi wakeel ne check nahi kiya. Public karne se pehle apne asli tareeqe ke saath milao aur [square brackets] wali jagah bharo.

Hum kya data rakhte hain

  • Account ka naam aur code, country aur language.
  • Aap ka journal: trades, setups, starting balance, deposit/withdraw, notes.
  • AI ki chats aur AI persona ki settings.
  • Plan, aur agar admin ne block kiya ho to wo status.

Ye data kahan rehta hai

Ye data Google Firebase (Firestore) par rakha jata hai. Aap ke device par kuch settings (theme, graphics, awaaz ki settings, chart ke indicators) browser ke localStorage mein save hoti hain.

AI

Jab aap AI se baat karte ho, aap ka sawal aur (agar aap ne "AI ko mera journal dekhne do" on rakha ho to) aap ke trades ka khulasa Google Gemini ko bheja jata hai. Aap wo option band kar sakte ho. Agar aap ne apni ElevenLabs key daali hai, to jawab ka text awaaz banane ke liye ElevenLabs ko jata hai. Awaaz sunne ke liye mic ki baat browser ki speech service tak jati hai.

Hum kya nahi karte

  • Hum aap se broker ka (Exness/MT5 ka) login ya password nahi maangte.
  • Hum aap ka data bechte nahi.

Surakhsha (sach baat)

Hum data ko surakshit rakhne ki koshish karte hain, lekin koi system 100% safe nahi hota. [Yahan apni asli security ki halat likho. Jab tak accounts Firebase Authentication par nahi hain, ye na likho ke passwords encrypted hain.] Apna code kisi ke saath share mat karo.

Aap ke haq

Aap apna journal "Backup" se export kar sakte ho. Account ya data hatwane ke liye rabta karo: [apna email ya WhatsApp number likho].
